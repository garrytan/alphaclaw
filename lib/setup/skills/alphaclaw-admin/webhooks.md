Named inbound HTTP hooks that receive payloads at `/hooks/<name>` (authed as a Bearer or `x-openclaw-token` header: hooks created since v0.10.247 take their own token, shown once to the person who created the hook and masked for you; older hooks may still take the shared `WEBHOOK_TOKEN`), optionally forward to a configured destination, and can expose an OAuth callback alias at `/oauth/<callbackId>`. Deliveries must be POST (any other method gets 405); the OAuth alias forwards every provider callback param (`code`, `state`, `oauth_verifier`, `realmId`, `shop`, `iss`, ...) (query, JSON or a `form_post` body) except `message`, `wakeMode`, `name` and `token` (a callback on the gateway's built-in `wake` or `agent` hook gets 404), and takes at most 10 calls a minute per id. AlphaClaw checks the token itself before forwarding (wrong or missing: 401; 20 failures a minute from one client: 429). OpenClaw's built-in `/hooks/agent` and `/hooks/wake` answer 404 unless the owner turns them on under Webhooks → OpenClaw built-in hooks; turning one on, minting its own token or reopening the shared token on it is for humans only (you get 403). A hook can also verify its sender (Circleback, Quo, Verkada or Standard Webhooks signatures, or its own token); `webhook.ingress.preset` in the detail says which (`token` means the shared token). Setting it up, its secrets and `/api/webhooks/:name/ingress*` are humans only (403 for you), and so is adding an OAuth callback alias to a hook whose preset is not `token`. A signed hook's transform gets only the signed body and signed headers: no query string, no other headers. A hook off the shared token (`ingress.preset` is not `token`, which includes every new hook) belongs to the owner: you get 403 deleting it or adding or rotating its OAuth callback alias, its callback URL is masked for you, and only an alias a person made or rotated while the hook was off the shared token delivers to it (others get 403 and the owner is told to rotate it once). Read endpoints also surface per-hook delivery health and the recorded request log. Gotcha: every mutation (create, update-destination, delete) runs `alphaclaw git-sync` and marks a restart as required, so changes are not live until the next restart — and the system-managed `gmail` hook is protected and returns 409 on delete. Transform-file conventions (the `hooks/transforms/<name>/<name>-transform.mjs` shape used to reshape payloads before forwarding) live in the `## Webhooks` section of the workspace's `hooks/bootstrap/AGENTS.md` (merged there from AlphaClaw's TOOLS.md template at workspace sync).
